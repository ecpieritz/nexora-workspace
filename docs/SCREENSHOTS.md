# Product screenshots

The portfolio presentation should include these responsive views when the application is deployed:

1. Authentication — sign in and sign up.
2. Workspace dashboard — summary, reports and recent orders.
3. Invoice management — list, actions and invoice preview.
4. Task management — list, board and timeline.
5. Calendar — day, month and year views.
6. Customer analytics — list, details and editor.
7. Product analytics — performance dashboard and product creation drawer.

Store exported images in `docs/screenshots/` using descriptive kebab-case names. Screenshots are intentionally not generated from local mock credentials or committed with browser chrome.
